## SECTION 6: CE CERTIFICATION — WHAT IT MEANS AND HOW TO VERIFY

### Why CE Matters for Israel

Israel adopted EU Cosmetics Regulation (EC No. 1223/2009) and aligns consumer electronics safety with EU standards. CE-marked electrical products enter Israel with minimal customs friction. Non-CE products can be held at customs and rejected.

### The CE Problem with AliExpress

Many AliExpress sellers put a "CE" logo on product photos without having actual documentation. This is a known issue. The CE logo alone is worthless — you need the certificate.

### How to Verify CE (for Every Electrical Item)

**Step 1:** Message supplier through AliExpress chat:
> "Hello, I need to ship this product to Israel/EU. Can you provide the CE certificate and Declaration of Conformity (DoC) as PDF files? My customers require this documentation."

**Step 2:** If supplier sends a PDF:
- Check the document has: product name, model number, manufacturer address, notified body number (if required), date
- Cross-check model number on certificate vs. product listing
- A real CE certificate looks like an official document, not a branded one-pager

**Step 3:** If supplier cannot provide PDF:
- Do not use this supplier for electrical items
- Find an alternative supplier with documentation

**Step 4:** Request IEC 62368-1 (audio/video/IT equipment) or IEC 60335 (household electrical appliances) test report
- Most cannot provide this for $20 drills
- If they can, it's a strong trust signal
- If not, use the CE DoC only and accept slightly higher risk

### Specific Concern: The $16.49 MelodySusie EU Drill

This drill is an older model (item/32825193664). MelodySusie is a real brand with real products, but their AliExpress-listed items may be grey market or older SKUs. Before using:
1. Verify it's an authentic MelodySusie product (not a counterfeit using the brand name)
2. Check that CE documentation is available
3. Order 1 sample and test for 30 minutes
