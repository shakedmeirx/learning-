## DISCOUNT STRATEGY

### Rule: Never Discount Kit Price Permanently

Permanent discounts train customers to wait for sales and devalue the brand. Use targeted discounts only:

| Trigger | Discount | Mechanic | Limit |
|---------|----------|----------|-------|
| First purchase | 10% off Kit 1 | Exit-intent popup | First order only |
| Abandoned cart (1 hr) | ₪20 off | Email automation | Kit 2 only |
| Kit 1 → Kit 2 upsell | ₪30 off | Email Day 30 | Existing customers |
| Holiday (Rosh Hashana, Hanukkah) | ₪30–50 off Kit 2 | Seasonal campaign | 7–10 day window |
| Influencer code | 10% | UTM-tracked code | Per influencer |
| Refer a friend | ₪30 each | Referral program | No limit |

### What Not to Do

- ❌ Site-wide "SALE" banners that devalue the brand
- ❌ Daily deals / flash sales that train bargain hunting
- ❌ Discounts on Kit 3 — preserve premium perception
- ❌ "SALE" crossed-out prices that look fake (Israeli consumers notice)
