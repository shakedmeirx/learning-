## JOURNEY OVERVIEW

Three personas, one overarching journey structure. Each stage documents touchpoints, emotional state, pain points, opportunities, and drop-off risks.

---

## JOURNEY: THE CURIOUS BEGINNER (Primary Persona)

### Stage 1: AWARENESS

**Customer goal:** Discover that a home nail kit is a real, viable option
**Emotional state:** Curious, slightly overwhelmed, mildly skeptical ("is this actually good quality?")

**Touchpoints:**
- TikTok: Sees a cat-eye nail transformation video with Hebrew audio
- Instagram Reels: "Look what I made at home" — a friend or influencer post
- Facebook: Scrolls past a sponsored ad showing kit unboxing
- Search: "ערכת מניקור ביתית" on Google

**Pain points at this stage:**
- Has no mental model of what a "home nail kit" contains
- Assumes anything from China = low quality / dangerous
- Doesn't know Hebrew-language stores exist for this

**Opportunities:**
- Be the first Hebrew result for "ערכת ציפורניים ביתית"
- Run TikTok content showing the EXACT question she has: "מה צריך כדי להתחיל?"
- Use Facebook ads with pain-point hooks: "חיפשת מקדחה ב-AliExpress ולא ידעת מה לבחור?"

**Key metric:** Impressions, reach, profile visits
**Drop-off risk:** Doesn't stop scrolling if hook isn't immediately Hebrew and visually clear

---

### Stage 2: CONSIDERATION

**Customer goal:** Evaluate if this store is trustworthy and if the products will work in Israel
**Emotional state:** Interested but cautious ("what if it doesn't work with Israeli plugs?", "what if it never arrives?")

**Touchpoints:**
- Product page: Reads Hebrew description, checks plug type, looks for CE mention
- Reviews section: Reads what other customers say (if exists)
- FAQ section: "האם המוצר עובד בישראל?", "כמה זמן לוקח המשלוח?"
- Instagram profile: Checks how many posts, if content looks real
- WhatsApp: May send a pre-purchase question

**Pain points at this stage:**
- Product page doesn't explicitly confirm EU plug / Israel compatibility
- No Hebrew reviews yet (new store)
- Doesn't know what CE certification means or why it matters
- Shipping time feels long (15–25 days) without reassurance

**Opportunities:**
- Lead product page with "עובד בישראל — תקע אירופי מובנה" as first bullet
- Create an FAQ section specifically addressing the 5 most common Israeli concerns
- Add a "מדריך לבחירת ערכה" (kit selection guide) as a trust builder
- Include a real WhatsApp button — visible, clickable, with business hours

**Key metric:** Time on site, FAQ engagement, WhatsApp pre-purchase questions
**Drop-off risk:** No Hebrew reviews + no obvious EU plug confirmation = exit

---

### Stage 3: DECISION

**Customer goal:** Complete the purchase with confidence
**Emotional state:** Ready to buy but needs one more reassurance ("I just need to confirm it's legit")

**Touchpoints:**
- Checkout page: Reviews total, sees PayPlus/Meshulam payment logo
- Discount trigger: First-visit popup or exit-intent offer
- Social proof: "15 נשים קנו את הערכה הזו השבוע" (live sales counter)
- Return policy: "14 יום להחזרה — ללא שאלות"
- Last push: Retargeting ad appears on Instagram showing the product they viewed

**Pain points at this stage:**
- Payment processor logos unfamiliar (not Visa/PayPal at top level)
- No phone number / WhatsApp button visible at checkout
- Doubt: "מה אם לא יגיע?"

**Opportunities:**
- Add Bit pay option (Israeli mobile payment — widely used, instant trust)
- Show delivery estimate explicitly: "מגיע לבית עד [date range]"
- Add exit-intent popup: "רגע לפני שעוזבת — 10% הנחה על הזמנה ראשונה"
- Guarantee text above checkout button: "תשלום מאובטח. 14 יום להחזרה."

**Key metric:** Checkout completion rate, cart abandonment rate
**Drop-off risk:** Unfamiliar payment processor + no visible return policy = abandon

---

### Stage 4: ONBOARDING (Post-Purchase)

**Customer goal:** Receive the kit, understand how to use it, achieve her first result
**Emotional state:** Excited to receive, then slightly anxious about using for the first time

**Touchpoints:**
- Order confirmation WhatsApp: Sent within minutes of purchase
- Shipping update: Tracking number via WhatsApp when item ships
- Package arrival: Unboxing experience — packaging matters
- First use: Does she have everything she needs? Are the instructions in Hebrew?
- Support if stuck: WhatsApp message "עזרה — המקדחה לא עובדת כמו שציפיתי"

**Pain points at this stage:**
- 15–25 day wait feels long; no proactive updates = anxiety
- Unboxing with no Hebrew instructions = frustration
- First time using a drill = intimidating without guidance
- If something doesn't work, she doesn't know who to call

**Opportunities:**
- Day 1 WhatsApp: "תודה על ההזמנה! הנה מספר המעקב שלך: [tracking]"
- Day 3 WhatsApp: "הערכה שלך בדרך! בינתיים — הנה סרטון להתחיל להתכונן"
- Include a small Hebrew Quick Start card in the package
- Send a "Welcome to the studio" video link via WhatsApp when package marked delivered
- Day 2 after delivery: "הגיע? 🎉 הנה סרטון ה-step-by-step שלנו לשימוש ראשון"

**Key metric:** First contact after delivery, support ticket rate, "thank you" responses
**Drop-off risk:** No proactive communication + no Hebrew guide = first-use failure = refund request

---

### Stage 5: RETENTION

**Customer goal:** Continue getting value; achieve increasingly better nail results; buy again
**Emotional state:** Satisfied but looking for growth ("what do I try next?")

**Touchpoints:**
- Instagram/TikTok: Sees new nail art tutorials she wants to try (from you or from influencers)
- WhatsApp sequence: 3 weeks after purchase — "מה עשית עם הערכה? שלחי לנו תמונה!"
- Email: "כיצד לשדרג מהסט הבסיסי לערכת הסטודיו"
- Return purchase: Kit 2 accessories replenishment (sanding bands, bits)

**Pain points at this stage:**
- Sanding bands/bits run out — she doesn't know where to reorder
- Wants to try cat-eye or chrome effect but doesn't have the right tools
- Doesn't know how to improve technique

**Opportunities:**
- 30-day email: "מוכנה לשלב הבא? הערכה שלנו עם מנורת UV"
- Create a "Consumables refill" product page (sanding bands, bits — small purchase, pure profit)
- Monthly "nail trends" WhatsApp message: "הטרנד החדש — קאט אי ורוד. הנה איך לעשות"
- User-generated content ask: "שלחי לנו את הציפורניים שלך — נפרסם!"

**Key metric:** Repeat purchase rate, WhatsApp response rate, review submission rate
**Drop-off risk:** No communication after delivery = one-and-done buyer

---

### Stage 6: ADVOCACY

**Customer goal:** Share her results with friends; recommend to others
**Emotional state:** Proud of her results, wants to share ("תראי מה עשיתי!")

**Touchpoints:**
- WhatsApp to friends: Shows her nails — "מה הסט שלך? הוא מ-הסטודיו הביתי!"
- Instagram post: Tags the brand in nail photo
- Review: Writes a ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ review on Shopify / Google
- Referral: Sends friend a link to the store

**Opportunities:**
- Referral program: "שלחי לחברה — את מקבלת ₪30 הנחה, היא מקבלת ₪30"
- Review ask: 14 days after delivery, WhatsApp: "מרוצה מהערכה? נשמח לחוות הדעת שלך ⭐"
- UGC repost: Always repost customer nail photos with credit — it validates and motivates others
- "Nail tutorial series" — invite top customers to record a 60-second tutorial for the brand

**Key metric:** Referral rate, review count, UGC submission rate, NPS
**Drop-off risk:** Never asked for review / referral = silent satisfied customer who tells no one

## DROP-OFF RISK MATRIX

| Transition | Drop-Off Cause | Fix |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Awareness → Consideration | Hook not Hebrew / no visual | Hebrew first-frame video content |
| Consideration → Decision | No EU plug confirmation | First product page bullet = plug spec |
| Decision → Purchase | Unfamiliar payment processor | Add Bit pay; show security badge |
| Purchase → Onboarding | 15-day silence | WhatsApp proactive update Day 1, 3, delivery |
| Onboarding → Retention | First-use failure | Hebrew quick-start card + delivery-day video |
| Retention → Advocacy | Never asked | 14-day review request + referral program |

## JOURNEY MAP — GIFT BUYER VARIANT

Gift buyer journey is shorter and more linear:

1. **Discovery:** Facebook ad "המתנה שהיא רצתה לעצמה" or Google "מתנה לאוהבת ציפורניים"
2. **Decision (fast):** Less research, higher trust threshold needed → show "מגיע מוכן. עם הנחיות. עובד בישראל."
3. **Purchase:** Gift message option at checkout ("הוסיפי כרטיס ברכה בעברית")
4. **Gifting:** The recipient is the real customer — she becomes an advocate if experience is great
5. **Return buyer:** Gift buyer becomes a self-buyer after positive recipient feedback

**Key difference:** Gift buyer converts faster but needs stronger plug/Israel compatibility reassurance and a gift presentation option (gift box or gift card at checkout).
