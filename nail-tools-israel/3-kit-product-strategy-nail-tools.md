# SECTION 1: STRATEGIC OVERVIEW

## Why These 3 Kits

A single product fails in the Israeli home nail market for two reasons: the market is split between women who have never touched a nail drill and women who already own one but want better tools. A 3-tier kit system solves both problems, plus creates a natural upsell ladder that increases average order value over time.

**The three-tier logic:**

```
KIT 1 → Awareness / Entry                No electrical risk. Low price. Low mental barrier.
KIT 2 → Hero / Volume Driver             The complete home salon. This is your primary revenue.
KIT 3 → Upsell / Aspirational           For serious hobbyists. High AOV, high LTV signal.
```

A customer who buys Kit 1 and loves it is a pre-qualified Kit 2 buyer. A Kit 2 buyer who starts doing nails for friends is a pre-qualified Kit 3 buyer. The brand makes money on Kit 2, builds loyalty on Kit 1, and builds margin on Kit 3.

## Kit Roles

| Role | Kit | Why |
|------|-----|-----|
| **Ad hero** | Kit 2 | Best price-to-value ratio for Facebook demos. $35–50 landed cost → ₪349 sell = clear margin + strong demo content. |
| **Acquisition / low-barrier entry** | Kit 1 | Run as a lower-cost ad to capture hesitant buyers. Natural upsell funnel to Kit 2. |
| **High-AOV / social proof** | Kit 3 | Position as the "full setup." Drives aspirational purchase from nail hobbyists who do nails for friends. |
| **Launch first** | Kit 2 | Clearest demo, widest target audience, best margin structure. |
| **Launch second** | Kit 1 | Adds lower-cost entry point once Kit 2 ad pixel has data. |
| **Launch last / Month 2** | Kit 3 | Needs more trust and social proof from reviews before justifying higher price. |

## Products Deliberately Excluded (and Why)

| Product | Why Excluded |
|---------|-------------|
| Gel/nail polish | UN Class 3 flammable, cannot ship via ePacket/standard air |
| Acetone / remover | Flammable liquid, same shipping restriction |
| Primer / dehydrator | Chemical cosmetic, requires Israeli registration |
| Base coat / top coat | Flammable, regulatory, shipping |
| Nail glue | Cyanoacrylate — flammable, health risk without instruction |
| Cuticle oil | Liquid cosmetic — shipping complexity, regulatory gray zone |
| Acrylic powder | Dust inhalation risk without proper ventilation — support liability |
| Nail forms (for extensions) | Requires liquid products to be useful — incomplete product without excluded items |
| UV lamp (Kit 1) | Electrical item adds unnecessary cost and complexity to entry kit |

# SECTION 4: PRODUCT SOURCING INSTRUCTIONS

## ELECTRICAL ITEMS — Universal Safety Rules

Before sourcing any electrical item (drill, lamp, dust collector):

**Required documentation from supplier:**
1. **CE Certificate** — Must show the product (not just the brand) on the certificate. Ask for the actual certificate PDF, not just a sticker photo.
2. **EU Plug confirmation** — Type C (two round pins) is the Israeli standard. Confirm this is included, not just an adapter.
3. **Declaration of Conformity (DoC)** — Legal document supplier signs. Most reliable AliExpress suppliers for CE products have this.
4. **Product test report** — Optional but ideal. Ask for IEC 60335 or equivalent safety test report.
5. **RoHS compliance** — Restriction of hazardous substances. Standard for electronics in EU/Israel.

**Red flags to filter out:**
- "CE" logo on product images but no actual certificate available
- Supplier says "CE certified" but cannot provide PDF documentation
- Reviews mentioning: "smell of burning," "gets very hot," "plug melted," "stopped working after 2 uses," "motor is very loud/weak"
- Products with 0–200 reviews (too few to assess quality)
- Products listed at suspiciously low prices (under $8 for a drill — this is a race-to-bottom product)
- UK/US plug offered as "standard" — confirm Type C Israeli/EU compatibility

**Before listing: order 1 sample of every electrical item.** Run it for 30 minutes. Check for heat, vibration, noise, plug stability. Do not list based on supplier photos alone.

# SECTION 11: FINAL RECOMMENDATION

## Which Kit to Launch First

**Launch Kit 2 (ערכת הסטודיו הביתי) first.** It is the hero kit, has the widest appeal (every woman who does gel at home is a potential buyer), and has the best demo potential for Facebook and TikTok. The cost-comparison hook ("₪120 in salon every 2 weeks vs. ₪349 once") is immediately understood and emotionally resonant.

## Which Kit is the Hero

**Kit 2.** No ambiguity. It is priced in the impulse-purchase range for the target demographic, has a clear complete-solution story, and generates enough margin to fund paid acquisition.

## Which Kit to Delay

**Delay Kit 3 until Month 2.** It requires more trust before a consumer spends ₪469 with a brand they've just discovered. Let reviews and social proof from Kit 2 buyers build first. Then launch Kit 3 as an "upgrade" — and Kit 2 buyers become your best Kit 3 audience.

## 7-Day Action Plan

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **Day 1** | Order 1 sample of each electrical item (drill + lamp). Place order today. Begin supplier qualification checklist while waiting. |
| **Day 2** | Set up Shopify store. Install Hebrew theme (Dawn or custom RTL). Configure PayPlus or Meshulam payment. Set currency to NIS. |
| **Day 3** | Write Hebrew product page for Kit 2 using copy from this document. Install Judge.me. Import AliExpress reviews for drill and lamp. |
| **Day 4** | Set up WhatsApp Business account. Add WhatsApp link to store header and footer. Create auto-reply message. |
| **Day 5** | Create first TikTok/Instagram Reel: 30-second unboxing + demo of drill and lamp. Post organically with Hebrew hashtags. |
| **Day 6** | Launch Facebook Advantage+ Shopping campaign: ₪55/day ($15), Women 22–45, Israel, automatic placements. Use video from Day 5. |
| **Day 7** | Monitor first ad results. Check CTR and initial engagement. Do NOT increase budget before Day 10. Post second organic video. |

## What to Test Before Spending Real Ad Budget

1. **Sample your own products.** Run the drill for 30 minutes. Check the lamp. Use them on your own nails if possible. If anything feels off — wrong supplier.
2. **Test your checkout flow from a mobile phone.** Hebrew RTL checkout, NIS pricing, payment works. Walk the full purchase path before the first ad is live.
3. **Test your WhatsApp response.** Send yourself a message and confirm the auto-reply fires and your number receives it correctly.
4. **Test one Hebrew influencer DM.** Before spending money on ads, reach out to 5 nano-influencers offering a free kit. If even one posts organically, you have social proof before ad budget is needed.

## Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Hurts | What to Do Instead |
|---------|-------------|-------------------|
| Sourcing electrical items without CE documentation | Liability risk, import risk, customer safety | Request CE PDF from every electrical supplier before ordering |
| Including gel polish, remover, or primer in any kit | Flammable shipping = orders seized | Kits are tools only. Period. |
| Launching all 3 kits on Day 1 | Splits ad budget, no product wins | Kit 2 first. 30 days. Then add Kit 1 and Kit 3. |
| Writing Hebrew copy via Google Translate | Sounds robotic, loses trust immediately | Use the Hebrew copy in this document, then get 1 native speaker to review before publishing |
| Expecting 3–5 day delivery | Israel is 15–25 days from China | Set this expectation clearly at checkout and in ads. Underpromise; overdeliver. |
| Scaling ad budget before first sale | Wastes budget on unvalidated creative | Wait for 1 purchase per ₪100–150 ad spend before scaling. If no purchase by ₪200 — change creative. |
| Ignoring post-purchase experience | Loses repeat buyers | WhatsApp follow-up + tracking number sent = repeat customer base |
