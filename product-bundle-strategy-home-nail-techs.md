# Product Bundle Strategy — Home Nail Tech Supplies Israel
## June 2026

---

## STRATEGIC CONTEXT

- **Import threshold:** $150 USD (raised from $75 in December 2025) — all bundles stay under this ceiling
- **Target customer:** Israeli home nail tech, already working, needs refill/restock not beginner kit
- **Shipping method:** ePacket / China Post (10–21 days), direct from AliExpress suppliers
- **No liquids:** zero gel polish, base coat, top coat, primer, cuticle oil, or any UN Class 3 product
- **Core positioning:** curated, Hebrew-explained, WhatsApp-supported monthly refill packs

---

## PRICING PHILOSOPHY

| Principle | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|
| Stay under ₪200 for consumable packs | Under psychological "session spend" threshold — feels like restocking, not shopping |
| Bundle at ₪79–₪149 for entry packs | Impulse-buy zone — no deliberation needed |
| Premium packs at ₪189–₪249 | One-time / seasonal buys — justify with specificity and photos |
| All packs under $75 item cost + $6 shipping | Comfortable clearance under $150 threshold with room for price increases |
| Never compete on price vs AliExpress | Price argument is a losing battle — compete on curation, speed, trust |

**Landed cost target:** Keep cost of goods + shipping under $25 per pack, sell at ₪89–₪199 = 3–5x margin.

## MONTHLY REFILL MODEL

**Recommended: Not a formal subscription (complex logistics), but a prompt-based refill system via WhatsApp.**

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1. Customer orders Bundle 1 | Marked as "refill customer" in WhatsApp |
| 2. Week 4 post-delivery | Proactive WhatsApp: "נגמרו לך שיופים?" |
| 3. Customer replies "כן" | Send payment link (bit.ly/bloom-refill or PayPal.me link) |
| 4. Collect payment, order from AliExpress | Ship within 2 business days of payment |
| 5. Track, update customer | WhatsApp shipping update |

**Goal:** Convert 40% of Bundle 1 buyers to 2nd+ order within 6 weeks.

## CUSTOMS / THRESHOLD COMPLIANCE

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| $150 USD threshold (Dec 2025) | All bundles are well under this at item cost alone |
| No VAT if under threshold | Customer pays 0 additional VAT on delivery |
| No invoice on package | Request from supplier: "gift" or low declared value |
| ePacket tracking | Confirm each supplier ships with tracking to Israel |
| Restricted products | No liquids, no batteries (e-drill or lamp = different channel) |

**Note on electrical items (drills/lamps):** NOT included in any of the above bundles. Electric drill requires CE certification, EU Type C plug, different shipping method (not ePacket). If added later, ship separately via DHL/FedEx Express, add CE documentation PDF, price separately at ₪350–600+.

## KEY SUPPLIER NOTES

1. **Order samples from 3 different AliExpress suppliers before selling any bundle.** This is especially critical for drill bits (quality varies 3x).
2. **Confirm ePacket ships to Israel** — most do, but verify with each new supplier.
3. **Request no invoice** on package, or declare as gift. Under new $150 threshold this matters less, but still best practice.
4. **Blinginbox** is the recommended rhinestone supplier (reputable, 5000+ reviews, consistent quality).
5. **Sanding bands** — test band durability. Cheap bands tear mid-use. Customer complaints will kill the brand.
