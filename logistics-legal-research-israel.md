## 2. SHIPPING METHOD: ePACKET / CHINA POST

### What Is ePacket?
ePacket is a postal service agreement between China Post and postal services of receiving countries (including Israel Post). It enables cheap, tracked shipping from China to Israel.

### Key Specs for Israel

| Spec | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Official name | China Post Air Parcel (ePacket) |
| Weight limit | 2 kg per package (most of our bundles = 200–500g) |
| Dimensions | Max 60cm × 40cm × 5cm |
| Delivery time | 10–21 business days (Israel) |
| Tracking | Yes — via Israel Post tracking or 17track.net |
| Cost | $4.50–$7.00 per package from China to Israel |
| Customs clearance | Usually automatic — customer doesn't need to do anything |
| Restricted items | No liquids, no batteries, no flammables |

### ePacket to Israel — Status
Israel Post is a participating country in ePacket agreements. Packages arrive to Ben Gurion sorting facility and are delivered by Israel Post (דואר ישראל) to customer address.

**Note on delays:** During Jewish holidays (Pesach, Rosh Hashana, Sukkot) Israel Post delivery slows significantly. Budget 25–30 business days during holiday months.

## 4. ISRAEL CONSUMER PROTECTION LAW

### Key Provisions Relevant to Our Business

**Consumer Protection Law (חוק הגנת הצרכן), 5741-1981, as amended:**

| Provision | What It Means |
|-----------|--------------|
| Right of cancellation — distance selling | Customer has 14 days to cancel an online purchase and receive full refund |
| Disclosure requirements | Must disclose: full business name, phone, address |
| Price transparency | Cannot charge hidden fees; final price must be clear |
| False advertising | Cannot make false claims about product origin, quality, or characteristics |

**14-Day Return Right (Specifically for Distance Sales):**
- Customer can cancel within 14 days of receiving goods, no reason required
- Seller must refund within 14 days of cancellation
- Exception: customized/personalized goods
- Exception: goods that cannot be returned for hygiene reasons (used cosmetic tools)

**Practical policy for us:**
- **Unopened products:** 14-day return, no questions
- **Opened products:** Case-by-case on WhatsApp — if product is defective, we refund. If just changed mind, assess case-by-case (the law may not require refund on opened hygiene-adjacent items, but good customer service = just accept it)
- **Defective products:** Always replace or refund, no time limit

## 5. ADVERTISING REGULATIONS

### Israeli Consumer Protection (Advertising)
- Cannot claim: "professional grade" without substantiation
- Cannot claim: "medical" or "therapeutic" benefits
- Cannot use: misleading comparative advertising without data
- Can claim: "tested by us", "carbide material", "EU standard" (with appropriate caveats)

**What to avoid in Hebrew ads:**
- "מוצר רפואי" / "מאושר רפואית" — no medical claims
- "מושלם" — technically legal but erodes trust
- "מוצר ישראלי" — if not manufactured in Israel
- "בוואצאפ אחד" overstatements — just describe honestly

## 6. BUSINESS REGISTRATION

### Options for Operating in Israel

| Structure | What It Is | When Relevant |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| עוסק פטור (Exempt Dealer) | Self-employed, under ₪120,000/year revenue | Best for launch phase |
| עוסק מורשה (Licensed Dealer) | Self-employed, VAT registered, any revenue | When revenue exceeds threshold |
| חברה בע"מ (Ltd) | Registered company | Only needed at significant scale |

**For launch phase (under ₪120,000/year = ~₪10,000/month):**
- Register as עוסק פטור with Israel Tax Authority (מס הכנסה)
- No need to charge customers VAT
- Simple annual tax filing
- Cost: free to register

**VAT note:** As an exempt dealer, you do NOT charge customers VAT. Once revenue exceeds the threshold, you must register as עוסק מורשה and add 17% VAT to all prices. Plan for this when revenue approaches ₪8,000–9,000/month.

**How to register:** Via the Israel Tax Authority website (my.gov.il) or through an accountant (~₪500–800 one-time).
