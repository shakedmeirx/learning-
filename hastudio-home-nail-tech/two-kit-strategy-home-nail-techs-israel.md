## CRITICAL VAT NOTE BEFORE READING

**The Israel personal import VAT exemption is currently $150 USD (not $75).**
It was raised from $75 to $150 effective January 1, 2026 (source: Haaretz, Globes, VATupdate, Herzog Law).
There is a pending High Court challenge from Israeli retailers — which failed to obtain an interim injunction —
so the $150 threshold is currently in force.

**However:** This strategy designs all kits to keep declared package value under $75 as a conservative safety buffer,
because: (a) legislation could be revoked by Knesset, (b) best practice in dropshipping is conservative declared values,
(c) it creates a stronger "zero customs fees" trust signal for customers.

See `vat-threshold-israel-dropshipping-notes.md` for full details.

## WHY 2 KITS (NOT 3)

The previous 3-kit strategy created a "what should I buy?" paralysis problem.
Israeli home nail techs are time-poor. A 2-kit ladder is simpler to advertise,
simpler to explain on WhatsApp, and simpler to upsell.

- **Kit 1** answers a single, urgent question: "נגמרו לך שיופים?"
- **Kit 2** answers the aspirational follow-up: "כדי לעבוד בצורה מסודרת ומקצועית יותר."

There is no competition between the two kits — Kit 1 creates Kit 2 buyers.

## KIT 1: חבילת הרענון החודשי (MONTHLY REFILL PACK)

### What It Is
A curated monthly refill of the dry consumables that every active home nail tech runs through fastest.
No tools, no art supplies — just the basics that run out.

### Why This Kit Wins as a Tripwire

1. **Solves an immediate, recurring pain** — sanding bands are the #1 consumable for gel nail removal
2. **Low price eliminates decision friction** — ₪89 is in impulse-buy range
3. **Creates repeat purchase cycle** — runs out in 4–6 weeks → automatic refill inquiry
4. **Proves product quality cheaply** — customer can verify band quality before committing to Kit 2
5. **Natural bridge to Kit 2** — "you love the sanding bands, now get the bits to match them"
6. **Easy to advertise** — single, relatable pain point: "נגמרו לך שיופים?"

## HOW KIT 1 SELLS KIT 2

### The Logic Chain
1. Customer is frustrated by running out of sanding bands mid-client
2. She finds Kit 1 ("חבילת הרענון החודשי") — an immediate, low-risk solution to her exact pain
3. She buys at ₪89 — impulse range, no deliberation
4. She receives quality bands + mandrels + files → **her problem is solved, trust is built**
5. She now has a positive association with the brand
6. **7–14 days later:** WhatsApp follow-up message (see funnel file): "שמחנו שקיבלת! ידעת שיש לנו חבילת שדרוג שכוללת ביטים קרביד + קישוטים + מחצלת?"
7. She compares ₪219 vs. buying 7 things separately from AliExpress and waiting 6 weeks for each
8. **She buys Kit 2.**

### Why This Funnel Works
- Kit 1 removes risk — she already knows the quality
- Kit 2 solves a different (larger) pain: "I want to look more professional, work faster, offer art"
- No overlap — Kit 1 is consumables, Kit 2 is tools + art + consumables upgrade
- The WhatsApp relationship is already established — no cold sale
- The price jump from ₪89 to ₪219 feels proportional to the value jump

No Israeli competitor currently offers:
- A curated Kit 1 tripwire under ₪100 specifically for home nail techs
- A Hebrew-explained bundle combining tools + art supplies under ₪220
- A WhatsApp-first, two-kit funnel model

AliExpress cannot offer: curation, Hebrew guidance, bundled shipping, or WhatsApp support.
Israeli professional stores cannot offer: home-tech pricing, sub-₪100 consumable packs, or active funnel sales.

**This gap is real, uncontested, and actionable.**
