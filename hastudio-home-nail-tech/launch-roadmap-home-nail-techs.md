## PRE-LAUNCH (DAYS 1–14)

### Week 1: Product Validation

**Day 1–2: Order product samples**
- [ ] Order Bundle 1 components from 3 different AliExpress suppliers
  - Sanding bands 100pcs (3 suppliers, lowest/mid/top price range)
  - Mandrel rods (2 suppliers)
  - Disposable files (2 suppliers)
- [ ] Order Bundle 3 components (rhinestones, foil, stickers, chrome powder)
- Total sampling budget: ~$60–80 (₪220–300)

**Day 3–5: Test products**
- [ ] Test sanding bands: compare durability, check if bands tear mid-use
- [ ] Test chrome powder: dry test, shimmer quality, coverage
- [ ] Test rhinestones: confirm flatback (not hotfix), check AB quality
- [ ] Document: photo each product, note which supplier won

**Day 5–7: Order Bundle 2 samples (drill bits)**
- [ ] Order carbide bit set from 3 suppliers ($15–20 total)
- [ ] Test: sharpness day 1, heat generation after 10 min use, durability
- [ ] Only sell bits that passed heat test
- [ ] Wait for sample delivery before adding Bundle 2 to live catalog

**Decision gate:** Do NOT launch Bundle 2 until bits pass heat test. Launch with Bundles 1 + 3 only if bits haven't arrived.

**Day 10–11: Instagram setup**

**Day 11–12: Landing page (optional for launch)**

**Day 12–14: Soft launch prep**

|--------|--------|---------|
| Instagram profile visits | 150 | 50 |
| WhatsApp inquiries | 10 | 3 |
| Orders placed | 3–5 | 1 |
| Revenue | ₪300–500 | ₪90 |

**Daily tasks:**

- [ ] Post 2–3 Stories per day

### Days 22–30: Conversion Push

**Goals:**
- Reach 10 cumulative orders
- First repeat inquiry (someone who bought in week 1 already asking about refill)
- Start collecting UGC (ask first 5 buyers for photo/video)

**Key tasks:**
- [ ] Follow up with all buyers at Day 7 post-order: "הכל בסדר? יצא המשלוח — יגיע בעוד X ימים."
- [ ] Send refill nudge to week-1 buyers at Day 21: "נגמרו שיופים? 😄"
- [ ] Launch Hook 2 ad (AliExpress comparison)
- [ ] Add Bundle 2 if bits passed quality test
- [ ] Post first customer UGC (with permission)
- [ ] Review: which bundle sold most? Double down on it.

**End of Month 1 decision gate:**

| Outcome | Action |
|---------|--------|
| 10+ orders, positive feedback | Scale ads to ₪600/month in Month 2 |
| 5–9 orders, good feedback | Continue at same pace, improve ad creative |
| < 5 orders | Diagnose: is it traffic (CTR problem) or conversion (offer problem)? Adjust before scaling |
| 0 orders after ₪300 ad spend | Stop, do WhatsApp outreach to 20 nail techs manually before spending more |

## DECISION TREE: WHEN TO SCALE VS. PAUSE

```
After 30 days:

ROAS > 3.0 AND > 15 orders → Scale ads 2x, add 2 more bundles
ROAS 2.0–3.0 AND > 10 orders → Maintain pace, improve creative
ROAS 1.0–2.0 AND > 5 orders → Pause ads, improve offer/copy, retry
ROAS < 1.0 OR < 5 orders after ₪400 spend → Do NOT scale
    → Manual outreach 20 nail techs via DM/WhatsApp
    → Test different hooks
    → Reconsider: is the pain point real? Is the price right?
```
