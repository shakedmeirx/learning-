## CPA TOLERANCE

**Kit 1 max CPA (break-even):** ₪50
**Kit 1 target CPA:** ₪35–₪40
**Kit 2 max CPA (break-even):** ₪129
**Kit 2 target CPA (direct):** ₪60–₪80
**Kit 2 CPA via Kit 1 upsell:** ₪0 (no ad spend for upsell — WhatsApp sequence)

**Key insight:** If Kit 2 is sold primarily via WhatsApp upsell (not paid ads), the effective CPA for Kit 2 is just the cost of acquiring the Kit 1 customer. Target: ₪40 CPA for Kit 1 → 30% convert to Kit 2 → blended CPA for Kit 2 = ₪133. Still profitable.

## SHOULD KIT 1 HAVE FREE SHIPPING?

**Recommendation: Include free shipping in the price. Do not charge separately.**

Reasoning:
- Israeli online shoppers are accustomed to free shipping from Israeli stores
- Showing ₪89 + ₪15 shipping creates friction and comparison anxiety
- ePacket cost ($5) is already baked into our landed cost
- "משלוח חינם" is a conversion-boosting trust signal
- Margin is sufficient even at ₪79 with free shipping (~51%)

**Kit 2:** Free shipping included. "אין עלויות מסתורות" → WhatsApp CTA.

## PAYMENT METHODS

**Primary: Bit (ביט)**
- Zero fee for personal transfers
- ~1.5% fee via business account
- Most common mobile payment in Israel
- No cart abandonment — instant payment via WhatsApp link

**Secondary: Bank transfer (העברה בנקאית)**
- Zero fee
- Slight friction — customer needs to make manual transfer
- Good fallback for customers without Bit

**Optional: PayPal.me link**
- 3.4% + $0.30 fee — expensive
- Use only for customers who insist on card payment
- Don't promote, just have available

**Future (Month 3+): Cardcom or Meshulam (Israeli credit card gateway)**
- 2–3% fee + monthly subscription
- Required when WhatsApp volume gets too high to manage
- Enables card payments via Shopify or standalone checkout page

## PRICING STRATEGY: WHEN TO RAISE OR LOWER

### When to Lower Kit 1 to ₪79 (Promo)
- Launch week: introduce at ₪79 "מחיר פתיחה" for first 30 days
- Facebook ad A/B test: ₪79 vs ₪89 to see conversion rate difference
- If Kit 1 CPA > ₪60 after ₪300 ad spend → try ₪79 to improve CTR

### When to Raise to ₪99
- After 20+ positive WhatsApp reviews / UGC collected
- If sell-through rate is > 80% in first month (demand exceeds supply)
- Add "פרמיום" variant: include 1 ceramic bit as bonus — justify price increase

### Kit 2 Pricing Logic
- ₪199 feels under ₪200 — psychological anchor
- ₪219 signals quality without feeling expensive vs. salon prices
- Do NOT go above ₪249 without adding significant perceived value (e.g., bit organizer, practice fingers)
- If Kit 2 sales are slow: offer "Kit 1 + Kit 2 bundle" at ₪269 (saves ₪39)

## BUNDLE DISCOUNT STRATEGY (Kit 1 + Kit 2)

**"חבילת הכל" = Kit 1 + Kit 2 together at ₪269**
- Standard total: ₪89 + ₪219 = ₪308
- Bundle price: ₪269 (saves ₪39 = 13% discount)
- Bundle landed cost: ~₪39 + ₪90 = ₪129
- Bundle gross profit: ₪140 (~52% margin)
- Single shipping: may be able to combine in one package (~$9–10 shipping vs $12 separate)
- **Use case:** Customer who sees both kits and can't decide — bundle closes the sale faster

## FUNNEL: KIT 1 → KIT 2

### Full Sequence with Timing

```
DAY 0: Customer buys Kit 1 via WhatsApp
  → Immediate WhatsApp confirmation: "קיבלנו! מזמינות מהספק ושולחות תוך יומיים. 📦"

DAY 2: Shipping update
  → WhatsApp: "החבילה שלך יצאה. מספר מעקב: [XXX] — אפשר לעקוב כאן: 17track.net"

DAY 14 (estimated delivery):
  → WhatsApp: "הגיעה? 😊 תספרי לנו אם הכל בסדר"
  → Goal: get a reply, confirm delivery, build relationship

DAY 21 (1 week after delivery):
  → WhatsApp: "איך השיופים? 😄 אם את מרוצה — יש לנו חבילת שדרוג שכוללת ביטים קרביד + ריינסטונס + כרום + מחצלת. תרצי לשמוע?"
  → First Kit 2 soft mention — question format, not hard sell

DAY 28 (2 weeks after delivery):
  → If no response to Day 21 message:
  WhatsApp: "📦 חבילת הסטודיו הביתי — ₪219 כולל הכל: ביטים קרביד, ריינסטונס, כרום, פויל, ומחצלת. [תמונה]. רוצה שנשלח?"

DAY 35 (refill window opens):
  → WhatsApp: "נגמרו שיופים? 🤔 חבילת רענון חדשה מוכנה — ₪89. רוצה שנזמין?"
  → Parallel: mention Kit 2 again briefly: "או שלחי אחת גדולה עם הביטים — ₪219"
```
