## SECTION 2: PRODUCT VALIDATION (BLOCKING — DO ON YOUR MAC)

### ❓ Q1: Which AliExpress sanding band supplier is best quality?
**Status:** BLOCKING for Kit 1
**Manual check:**
1. Open aliexpress.com on your Mac
2. Search: "nail sanding band 100pcs"
3. Find item 1005001446533060 (confirmed $1.48/100pcs) — check current price and availability
4. Find 2 more competing listings with 5000+ orders
5. Order all 3 as samples — mark your "test batch 1/2/3"
6. When they arrive: put each band on a mandrel, run on practice tip for 3–5 minutes
7. Check: does the band stay on mandrel? Does it tear? How many strokes before it loses grip?
8. Keep the supplier whose bands last longest → that's your Kit 1 supplier

### ❓ Q2: Which carbide bit supplier passes the heat test?
**Status:** BLOCKING for Kit 2
**Manual check:**
1. Search AliExpress: "carbide nail drill bits set 6pc"
2. Find 3 suppliers with 3000+ orders and different price points ($3 / $5 / $8 range)
3. Order all 3
4. When they arrive: run each bit at medium speed on a practice tip for 5 minutes
5. After 5 min: touch the metal shank near the head — is it uncomfortably hot?
6. PASS: warm but can hold. FAIL: too hot to hold for 3 seconds.
7. Only include passing bits in Kit 2

**Estimated cost:** $30–40 for 3 sample sets

### ❓ Q3: Do the mandrels fit the sanding bands?
**Status:** BLOCKING for Kit 1
**Manual check:**
1. When samples arrive, test: slide a sanding band onto the mandrel
2. Should fit snugly — not fall off with light shake, not require force
3. Most standard sanding bands use 3/32" inner diameter mandrels
4. If mismatch: confirm both items spec 3/32" in their AliExpress listing

### ❓ Q4: Does the chrome powder work on no-wipe gel?
**Status:** BLOCKING for Kit 2 (if including chrome powder)
**Manual check:**
1. Order chrome powder 3-color set (rose gold/silver/aurora) from AliExpress
2. Apply a layer of no-wipe gel to a practice tip, cure under UV lamp
3. Apply chrome powder with silicone sponge applicator
4. Check: does it create a mirror/chrome finish? Any streaking or patchiness?
5. If it works → include in Kit 2
6. If patchy → test with 2 different chrome powder brands before deciding

**Note:** Chrome WILL NOT work on regular wipe-off gel. You need a no-wipe (inhibition-layer-free) gel. If you don't have no-wipe gel for testing, the test won't be valid.

## SECTION 6: LEGAL / ADMIN

### ❓ Q15: Is business registration as עוסק פטור needed before first sale?
**Status:** NON-BLOCKING for first few sales but should be done before Month 2
**Manual check:**
1. Go to my.gov.il / Israel Tax Authority (רשות המסים) online portal
2. Search: "פתיחת תיק עוסק פטור"
3. Threshold: if revenue will stay under ₪120,000/year (~₪10,000/month) → עוסק פטור
4. Process: online registration, takes ~1 week
5. Cost: free
6. Or: hire an accountant (~₪500–800 one-time for first-year setup)

---

### ❓ Q16: What Bit payment account is needed for business collections?
**Status:** BLOCKING if using Bit for payments
**Manual check:**
1. Open the Bit app on your phone
2. Check: is your Bit account linked to a personal or business bank account?
3. For business collections: you may need a business bank account with Bank Hapoalim / Leumi / Mizrahi
4. Fees for Bit business collections: ~1.5%
5. Ask your bank directly what's needed for monthly transfer volumes of ₪1,000–5,000
