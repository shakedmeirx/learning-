# Open Questions & Validation Plan — Home Nail Tech Supplies Israel
## June 2026

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

Each question is structured as:
1. **The question** — what we don't know yet
2. **Why it matters** — what decision it affects
3. **How to validate** — the specific action to answer it
4. **Timeline** — when you need the answer
5. **Blocking?** — does this block launch, or can you launch and learn?

## SECTION A: PRODUCT & SOURCING

### Q1: Which AliExpress supplier has the best sanding bands?
**Why it matters:** Sanding bands are the highest-repeat product. If they tear mid-use, customers won't return and will post complaints.
**How to validate:**
- Order same 100pcs lot from 3 different suppliers (total cost: ~$15–20)
- Test: wrap a band on mandrel, run on practice tip for 3 minutes
- Check: does it hold shape? Does it slide off the mandrel? Does it tear?
- The supplier whose bands survive 5 minutes on a rotating drill without tearing = your supplier.
**Timeline:** Before launching Bundle 1 (Day 1–7)
**Blocking:** YES — do not sell Bundle 1 until you have a tested supplier.

---

### Q2: Do the carbide bits actually pass a heat test?
**Why it matters:** Overheating bits cause burns. One customer complaint about burns ends this brand.
**How to validate:**
- Order bits from 3 suppliers ($15–20 total)
- Run each bit on a practice tip for 5 minutes at medium drill speed
- After 5 minutes: touch the bit shank — is it hot enough to burn skin?
- Pass = warm but not uncomfortable. Fail = too hot to hold.
- Only stock passing bits.
**Timeline:** Before launching Bundle 2 (Day 7–14)
**Blocking:** YES for Bundle 2. Bundle 1 can launch without it.

---

### Q3: Does ePacket from each supplier reliably reach Israel?
**Why it matters:** If a supplier's ePacket shipments don't arrive, you get refund requests and lose trust fast.
**How to validate:**
- When ordering your product samples, use ePacket shipping (not DHL)
- Track the shipment — does it arrive within 21 days?
- If a sample arrives in 10–15 days via ePacket → supplier is reliable for Israel
- If sample takes 25+ days or arrives via a different carrier → test another supplier
**Timeline:** Confirm with your sample orders (Day 1–21)
**Blocking:** Partially — if your first sample supplier delivers on time, you can launch. You validate others as you add bundles.

---

### Q4: What is the actual quality of AliExpress chrome powder?
**Why it matters:** Chrome powder is new for many Israeli home techs. If it doesn't work as expected, they'll blame the supplier (you).
**How to validate:**
- Order 3 different chrome powder sets
- Test on practice tips with a top coat-free gel surface (chrome requires no-wipe top coat to work)
- Check: does it transfer as a mirror finish? Any patchiness?
- Compare: rose gold, silver, aurora — which colors perform best?
- Include usage instructions with every Bundle 3 sale
**Timeline:** Before launch (Day 1–10)
**Blocking:** NO — you can launch Bundle 3 and include "instructions in the package" as a trust signal. But test first.

---

### Q5: Is the mandrel size compatible with the sanding bands?
**Why it matters:** If mandrel diameter doesn't match band inner diameter = unusable.
**How to validate:**
- Order both sanding bands and mandrels from same supplier
- Test fit: band should slide on snugly, not slip off during use
- Confirm: 3/32" shank mandrel (most common) fits bands you ordered
**Timeline:** Day 1–7 with first sample order
**Blocking:** YES — must confirm before selling Bundle 1.

## SECTION B: MARKET & CUSTOMER

### Q6: Is the home nail tech segment in Israel large enough to sustain a business?
**Why it matters:** If the segment is too small (< 5,000 potential customers), paid ads may not work.
**How to validate:**
- Run a ₪100 Meta awareness campaign targeting women 20–40 in Israel with interests in nail art, DIY beauty, מניקור
- Measure: audience size shown in Meta Ads (before running) — should be 100K+ for healthy targeting
- Check: Instagram hashtag volume for #נייליסטיתביתית, #ציפורנייםביתי, #מניקוריסטיתביתית
- Post one organic Instagram Reel about "הבעיה עם ציוד מאלי" and measure saves + comments
**Timeline:** Week 1–2
**Blocking:** NO — launch anyway, but monitor. If Instagram Reel gets < 50 views organically after 1 week, the niche may need a different channel.

---

### Q7: What is the actual willingness to pay?
**Why it matters:** ₪89 for 100 sanding bands may feel expensive or cheap depending on what customers compare to.
**How to validate:**
- In first WhatsApp conversations, note: do customers push back on price?
- Ask 3 early buyers: "כמה ציפית שזה יעלה?" before revealing price
- Run a poll on Instagram Stories: "כמה תשלמי על 100 שיופים מסודרים בעברית?" — options: ₪59 / ₪89 / ₪119 / יותר
**Timeline:** Month 1
**Blocking:** NO — launch at ₪89 and adjust based on feedback.

---

### Q8: What is the actual repeat purchase cycle?
**Why it matters:** Monthly refill model depends on customers running out in ~4 weeks.
**How to validate:**
- At Week 4, WhatsApp every Bundle 1 buyer: "נגמרו שיופים?"
- Track: how many said yes? How many reordered?
- Ask a few: "כמה לקוחות את עושה בשבוע?"
- If most have 2–4 clients/week = sanding bands last 3–5 weeks. If 6–8 clients/week = 2–3 weeks.
**Timeline:** Week 4–6
**Blocking:** NO — this is a learning question, not a launch blocker.

---

### Q9: Which ICP converts first — beginner (ICP A) or established (ICP B)?
**Why it matters:** Determines where to focus Month 2 content and targeting.
**How to validate:**
- In WhatsApp conversations, ask: "כמה לקוחות את עושה בשבוע?" (1–2 = ICP A, 3+ = ICP B)
- Tag each buyer in spreadsheet by ICP
- After 10 buyers: which ICP bought first bundle more?
**Timeline:** Month 1
**Blocking:** NO.

## SECTION C: LOGISTICS & OPERATIONS

### Q10: Can suppliers ship with no invoice / low declared value?
**Why it matters:** Even under the $150 threshold, packages with high declared values attract customs inspection.
**How to validate:**
- Message your tested supplier: "Can you ship as gift, no invoice, declared value $5–10?"
- Most AliExpress sellers will say yes — confirm before selling
- Test with your first sample order (mark as gift, declared value low)
**Timeline:** During sampling (Day 1–7)
**Blocking:** LOW RISK — threshold is $150 now, even at full value most bundles clear. But still confirm.

---

### Q11: What happens if a supplier runs out of stock?
**Why it matters:** Your business depends on AliExpress supplier inventory. If they run out, you can't fulfill orders.
**How to validate:**
- For each product in your bundles: identify 2 backup suppliers (save their store URLs)
- Order from both your primary and one backup to compare quality
- Don't commit to "in stock" guarantee to customers — instead say "מזמינים בתוך 2 ימי עסקים מתאריך ההזמנה"
**Timeline:** During sampling
**Blocking:** NO — but have backup suppliers identified before launch.

---

### Q12: What is the actual delivery time distribution to Israel?
**Why it matters:** "10–21 days" is a range — knowing the actual distribution helps set customer expectations accurately.
**How to validate:**
- Track every sample order from ship date to delivery date
- After 5 deliveries: average actual delivery time
- Update customer communications to reflect real experience (not just promised range)
**Timeline:** First 30–45 days
**Blocking:** NO — communicate the range until you have real data.

## SECTION D: BUSINESS MODEL

### Q13: Is WhatsApp-only sales sustainable at scale?
**Why it matters:** If you reach 50+ active buyers, managing orders via WhatsApp manually is time-intensive.
**How to validate:**
- Track: how many minutes per day do you spend on WhatsApp for orders?
- At what number of orders/week does it become a burden?
- Decision threshold: if orders > 15/week = consider Shopify
**Timeline:** Month 2–3
**Blocking:** NO at launch. Revisit at 60-day mark.

---

### Q14: Will Israeli home nail techs trust a new unknown brand?
**Why it matters:** Trust is the primary purchase barrier for a first-time Israeli supplier.
**How to validate:**
- Track first 10 WhatsApp conversations: how many ask "מי אתן? ממתי אתן פועלות?"
- Collect first 3 testimonials quickly — even informal WhatsApp messages
- Note: does adding a real phone number (visible on Instagram) improve inquiry rate?
**Timeline:** Month 1
**Blocking:** NO — but this informs trust-building content priority.
