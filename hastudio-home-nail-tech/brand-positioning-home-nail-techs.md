## CURRENT BRAND NAME EVALUATION: "הסטודיו הביתי"

**Verdict: Works — but needs a supply-focused tagline to pivot clearly.**

| Attribute | Assessment |
|-----------|-----------|
| Warmth / relatability | ✅ Strong — "ביתי" is warm and approachable |
| Professional signal | ✅ "סטודיו" elevates without pretension |
| Differentiation | ✅ Unique — no competitor uses this name |
| Works for supplies pivot | ⚠️ Slightly ambiguous — could still mean "buy a kit" |
| Memorability | ✅ Easy to say, easy to remember |
| Domain potential | ✅ hastudiohabeiti.co.il / nastudio.co.il |

**Conclusion:** Keep "הסטודיו הביתי" as the brand name. Modify the positioning line (tagline) to make it explicitly about supplies, not kits. The name is strong enough to grow with the brand.

### Positioning Statement

**For** Israeli home nail techs (נייליסטיות ביתיות) who do nails for friends and paying clients from home, **הסטודיו הביתי** is the only Hebrew-first supplier that offers curated small supply packs under the import threshold — with WhatsApp support and explanations in real Israeli Hebrew — so you can spend your time doing nails, not researching what to buy.

**Unlike** AliExpress (fragmented, no Hebrew, 3–6 weeks, no support) and Israeli professional stores (expensive, not curated for home techs), we understand exactly what a home nail tech needs every month.

### Tone of Voice

**5 Descriptors:**
1. **ישירה** (Direct) — says exactly what it is, no fluff
2. **חמה** (Warm) — like a more experienced friend who does nails
3. **מעשית** (Practical) — focused on what actually helps, not aspirational marketing
4. **כנה** (Honest) — about what we sell, what we don't sell, and why
5. **מכבדת** (Respectful) — treats the nail tech as a professional, not a beginner

**What it sounds like in practice:**

✅ DO: "נגמרו לך שיופים באמצע לקוחה? קורה. הנה חבילת refill של 200 בגריטים שאת הכי משתמשת בהם."
❌ DON'T: "ערכת השיוף המושלמת לכל הסטודיו שלך — מדהים ומקצועי!"

✅ DO: "הביטים האלה הם כרומיד אמיתי — מחזיקים 3 פעמים יותר מהזולים."
❌ DON'T: "ביטים פרימיום בסטנדרט יוקרתי!"

✅ DO: "לא מוכרות לק ג'ל — לא ניתן לשלוח נוזלים בדואר. הכל אצלנו הוא ציוד יבש."
❌ DON'T: [ignore the question]

### Visual Direction

**Overall aesthetic:** Professional-warm. Think: a real home studio that looks good but not overdone. Organized. Natural light. Real hands (not stock photos).

**Color palette:**
- Primary: מוקה עמוק (deep mocha) — #3D2B22 — professional, grounded
- Secondary: ורוד אבק בהיר (light dusty rose) — #E8C4B8 — feminine, soft
- Accent: שמפניה (champagne) — #C9A96E — quality signal, not fake gold
- Background: לבן-חם (warm white) — #FAF8F5 — clean, not clinical
- Success/CTA: ירוק-ווצאפ (WhatsApp green) — #25D366 — natural WhatsApp association

**Typography feel:** Clean, readable Hebrew. Assistant or Heebo. Not decorative fonts — this is a supply brand, not a spa.

**Photography style:**
- Real hands, real nails — not stock photos
- Studio setup shots: organized bit holders, swatch display, mat on table
- Before/after of supplies (new vs worn sanding bands)
- WhatsApp conversation screenshots (real-looking)
- Product flat-lays on warm white or marble background

**What NOT to do visually:**
- Purple gradients
- Gold glitter everywhere (looks cheap)
- Stock photo hands with impossible nails
- Dark/moody salon photography
- "Luxury" aesthetic — this is a practical supply brand

## TRUST SIGNALS

What makes a home nail tech trust a new supplier:

| Trust Signal | Implementation |
|--------------|---------------|
| "מה בדיוק יש בחבילה" — exact list | Every product page shows exact count, grit, size |
| Real customer photos | UGC from customers — not studio shots |
| WhatsApp number visible everywhere | Phone number on header, every product page, footer |
| "לא מוכרות נוזלים" — honest about scope | Dedicated section explaining what we DON'T sell |
| Transparent shipping time | "10–21 ימי עסקים — לא 3 ימים" |
| Specific specs (not just "professional grade") | Carbide bit = state the material, RPM range |
| Import threshold transparency | "כל חבילה מתחת ל-$150 — לא תשלמי מכס" |
| Returns / guarantee | Simple return promise — WhatsApp to arrange |

## COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATION ARGUMENTS

### Why us over AliExpress

**Their real objection:** "זה זול יותר באלי"
**Our honest answer:** "נכון. אבל באלי את מבלה 45 דקות מחפשת איזה ביטים לקנות, ואז מקבלת את זה אחרי 5 שבועות ולא יודעת אם זה טוב. אצלנו — חבילה ספציפית לנייליסטית ביתית, עם הסבר בעברית, ובוואצאפ אם יש שאלה."

Arguments:
1. **זמן** — we save 30–45 min of research per order
2. **ביטחון** — we pre-tested quality, she doesn't have to
3. **עברית** — all descriptions in Hebrew with real explanations
4. **תמיכה** — WhatsApp when something is wrong
5. **חבילה** — one delivery instead of 8 from different stores
6. **ידע** — we explain what each product is for, not just what it is

**Do NOT argue on price.** Accept the price difference and argue on value.

### Why us over Israeli professional stores

**Their real objection:** "אפשר לקנות בנאילה / אי-ניילס"
**Our honest answer:** "כן, ויש להם כל דבר. אבל הם לא בנו חבילות לנייליסטית ביתית — הם מוכרים מוצרים נפרדים. אצלנו יש חבילת refill חודשית — את יודעת בדיוק מה לקנות, כמה זה עולה, ואיך משתמשים בזה."

Arguments:
1. **Curation** — we select specifically for home nail tech needs
2. **Pack size** — small packs designed for 1 person, not salon bulk
3. **Price** — likely 20–40% cheaper than local stores for same items
4. **WhatsApp** — local stores don't offer this
5. **Hebrew explanations** — they have Hebrew UX but not Hebrew education
