## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VERDICT

**Verdict: TEST SMALL — GO with a staged two-kit test.**

- **First kit to test: Kit C — Nail Art / Trend Kit ("ערכת הטרנדים")** built around magnetic cat-eye tools + chrome powders + nail art accessories. Cheapest to sample (~$35), zero regulatory risk, zero flammable items, rides the #1 trend on TikTok right now (silver cat-eye: 12.7M+ plays, 135K monthly searches, still accelerating into 2026). Sell at ₪169.
- **Backup/scale kit: Kit B — Pro Beginner Kit ("ערכת המקצוענית")** anchored on the 3-in-1 manicure machine (35,000RPM drill + 48W lamp + dust collector, $11–26 from AliExpress). Sell at ₪449. Higher margin, higher perceived value, but carries electrical-import gray-zone risk — test after Kit C proves the audience.
- **The hard "no" list:** gel polish, base/top coat, builder gel, primer, acetone, nail glue (liquid), cuticle oil — all UN1263 Class 3 flammable liquids, cannot be dropshipped by air. Also avoid **cordless/rechargeable** lamps and drills (lithium battery = Class 9 dangerous goods). Corded only.
- **The price gap is enormous:** ColoresIsrael sells a beginner kit at **₪1,979**. Picaso sells branded starter kits at premium prices. Nail tech courses cost ₪2,000–6,000 and bundle kits "worth ₪5,100." Meanwhile the underlying hardware costs $25–40 landed. A ₪449 honest, curated, Hebrew-supported kit sits in an empty price band.
- **The structural constraint that shapes everything:** the DIY-gel-girl segment *needs liquids* you cannot ship. So the offer must be "all the hardware + dry consumables — buy your gel at any local store" (and that honesty becomes a trust asset, not a weakness).
- **VAT:** personal-import VAT exemption **reverted to $75 on June 2, 2026** (the $150 raise was temporary). Every kit below is designed to land with declared value under $70.
- **Minimum test budget: ~$330–450** (samples + content + first ads), inside your range.

## 2. MARKET VALIDATION

### Is there real demand?

**Globally — yes, confirmed:**
- Cat-eye gel polish is the dominant nail trend of early 2026: "cat eye nail polish" and "cat eye gel polish" are top TikTok keyword suggestions; cat-eye nails pull ~135,000 monthly searches and the trend is still accelerating (NAILSAMI 2026 guide, NailKnowledge Spring 2026).
- Chrome nails consistently show the highest search interest (peak 100 on Jan 3, 2026 per Accio trend data).
- The at-home/DIY movement is growing, "fueled by social media and the availability of user-friendly products" — press-ons peaked Dec 2025, gel polish search spiked Sept 2025.
- Why cat-eye matters for *content*: "cat eye nails catch light in a way almost nothing else does... the shimmer streak moves" — it is inherently filmable, which is exactly what a TikTok-first store needs.

**In Israel — yes, with strong price-pain evidence:**
- Salon economics drive the DIY motive: Israeli gel manicure price lists (noalee.co.il, inails.co.il 2026 price list) show recurring salon costs that a one-time kit purchase undercuts within 2–3 visits. Israel Hayom even covered a ₪2,000 single nail-build as a luxury phenomenon.
- Local kit prices are extreme: **ColoresIsrael "ערכה למתחילות" = ₪1,979**. Picaso sells a branded beginner gel kit (3 colors, base/top, primer, LED lamp, remover) at premium pricing.
- Nail tech courses cost **₪2,000–6,000** (SUNNAILS ₪2,900; range ₪1,500–10,000), and schools market "free kit worth ₪5,100" as the hook — proof that the *kit itself* is understood locally as a high-value object.
- The home-nail-tech segment is organized and commercially reachable (confirmed in `hastudio-home-nail-tech/` research: goodlak.co.il sells a "יומן נייליסטה" planner to this exact audience; hashtag #נייליסטיתביתית is active).

### Is it too competitive?
- **Local stores (naila, inails, nailbox, nail-plus, thenailshop, manicure-shop, adinails, topnail):** all sell individual professional products or expensive branded kits. None sell a curated, honestly-priced, Hebrew-educated starter kit in the ₪150–500 band (full profiles in `hastudio-home-nail-tech/competitor-research-v2-two-kit-lens.md`, re-verified this session).
- **AliExpress/Temu self-import:** the real competitor. 20–30% cheaper, 5–8 week waits, zero curation, zero Hebrew, zero support.
- **Conclusion:** competitive at the poles (premium local / cheap-fragmented Chinese), empty in the middle. Same gap found three times now across three research passes — that consistency is itself a signal.

## 4. PRODUCT SAFETY & SHIPPING — THE HARD RULES

| Product | Classification | Air-shippable (standard lines)? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gel polish / base / top / builder gel / primer | **UN1263 Class 3 flammable** (ethyl/butyl acetate solvents) | **NO** | ❌ NEVER include |
| Acetone / remover / nail glue (liquid) / cuticle oil | Class 3 / restricted liquids | NO | ❌ NEVER include |
| Cordless/rechargeable lamp or drill | **Class 9 — lithium battery DG** (UN38.3 report + MSDS required; 30% charge rule from Jan 1, 2026) | Only with DG docs | ❌ Avoid — use corded |
| **Corded** UV/LED lamp (48W) | Standard electronics | YES | ✅ with CE + EU plug |
| **Corded** nail drill / 3-in-1 machine | Standard electronics | YES | ✅ with CE + EU plug |
| Drill bits, mandrels, sanding bands | Metal/abrasive — no class | YES | ✅ Safe |
| Chrome/cat-eye powders, glitters | Dry metallic/mineral powder | YES | ✅ Safe (confirmed in prior research) |
| Magnetic cat-eye tools | Magnet + plastic | YES | ✅ Safe |
| Files, buffers, brushes, pushers, tips, stickers, foils, rhinestones | Inert | YES | ✅ Safe |
| Practice hand/fingers | Silicone/plastic | YES | ✅ Safe |
| Nail glue **tabs** (adhesive stickers) | Dry adhesive | YES | ✅ Safe alternative to liquid glue |

**Electrical-import note (be honest about this):** corded lamps/drills are not dangerous goods, but Israel regulates commercial import of electrical appliances (SII standards). Under the personal-import dropship model each customer is the importer, which is how thousands of these units already enter Israel monthly via AliExpress — but it's a gray zone for a *business*. Mitigations: (1) demand CE certificate **PDF** (not just a sticker) from the supplier, (2) EU Type C plug only, (3) low-voltage USB-powered lamps where possible (a 48W USB lamp avoids plug-standard issues entirely), (4) start with the non-electrical Kit C while you verify. This mirrors the constraint already documented in `nail-tools-israel/` research.

## 6. KIT STRATEGY

### Kit A — DIY Starter Kit | "ערכת הבית" (entry offer)
- **Target:** DIY girls replacing salon visits. **Pain:** salon cost; intimidation.
- **Contents:** 48W USB lamp, file/buffer set, cuticle pusher, dust brush, nail tips 100pc + adhesive tabs, sticker/foil pack, Hebrew quick-start cards.
- **Explicitly NOT included (say it on the page):** gel polish — "קני את הג'ל בכל חנות בארץ, אנחנו נסביר לך בדיוק מה לבקש." Honesty = trust = differentiation.
- Sourcing ~$13 / shipping ~$8 → landed ~$21 (₪79) | **Sell: ₪249** | margin ~68% | break-even CAC ~₪110
- **Main angle:** "כל החומרה לבית — בשליש ממחיר סלון אחד בחודש." **Objection:** "no gel included?" → counter with the local-gel guide as a *feature*.

### Kit B — Pro Beginner Kit | "ערכת המקצוענית" (hero / margin offer)
- **Target:** girls starting to take clients at home. **Pain:** course kits cost thousands; amateur setup.
- **Contents:** 3-in-1 machine (drill+lamp+dust collector), carbide bit set, sanding bands 100pc + mandrels, practice hand, tips 500pc + tabs, brush set, files/buffers, storage case, Hebrew bit-guide cards.
- Sourcing ~$38 / shipping ~$12 → landed ~$50 (₪188) | **Sell: ₪449** | margin ~58% | break-even CAC ~₪200
- Declared value $45–60 → safely under the $75 threshold.
- **Main angle:** "הערכה שמחזירה את עצמה אחרי 4 לקוחות." **Objections:** electrical trust → CE PDF shown on page, 30-day replacement promise; "drill scares me" → bit-guide video series.
- **Risk:** electrical gray zone (see §4). Launch *after* Kit C validates the audience.

### Kit C — Nail Art / Trend Kit | "ערכת הטרנדים" ⭐ FIRST TEST
- **Target:** both segments; trend-driven impulse buyers. **Pain:** cat-eye/chrome FOMO; local stores don't sell these tools cheaply.

- **Risk:** trend window. Mitigate: small orders, no deep stock (dropship = no stock anyway).

## 10. SUPPLIER OUTREACH TEMPLATES

**First message (Alibaba/AliExpress):**
> Hello, I run an online store in Israel selling nail kits. I'm interested in [product]. Before ordering samples: 1) Can you ship to Israel, and by which line (Yunexpress/4PX/CJ)? 2) Typical delivery time to Israel? 3) Does this product contain any liquid, alcohol, or battery? 4) For electrical items — do you have a CE certificate PDF and EU Type C plug version? 5) Sample price including shipping? 6) Can you declare "beauty tools, gift" at $25–40 value? Thank you.

**Follow-ups:** ask for (a) real photos/videos of the exact SKU ("not catalog renders — photos of the item you will ship"), (b) defect/replacement policy in writing, (c) MOQ for logo on box/pouch ("what is your MOQ for a custom insert card printed in Hebrew if I supply the PDF?"), (d) bundling: "can you pack these 5 SKUs in one box as a kit, one tracking number?", (e) dropship support: "can you ship single orders to my customers' addresses with no invoice inside?"

**Safety screener (send to every supplier, every electrical/powder item):**
> Please confirm in writing: 1) Is this item classified as dangerous goods for air transport (IATA)? 2) Does it contain a lithium battery? If yes — is it removable, and do you have the UN38.3 test report? 3) Does it contain any flammable liquid (gel, primer, glue, polish)? 4) Do you have an SDS/MSDS for the powders? We cannot accept any item that fails these checks.
