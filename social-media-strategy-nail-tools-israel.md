## CONTENT PILLAR FRAMEWORK (40/25/25/10 Rule)

### Pillar 1: Educational (40% of content)
*What it is:* Teach the audience something useful about nail care at home

| Content Type | Hebrew Caption Style | Visual |
|-------------|---------------------|--------|
| "איך משתמשים במקדחה לראשונה" | "מקדחה? לא מפחיד 🙅 הנה 3 שלבים פשוטים..." | Screen-record style demo |
| "מה זה CE ולמה זה חשוב" | "ראית את המדבקה CE? הנה מה שהיא אמורה להגיד..." | CE document close-up |
| "ההבדל בין מנורה 36W ל-48W" | "שאלה שמגיעה הרבה. הנה התשובה הכנה:" | Side-by-side comparison |
| "קאט אי ציפורניים — מדריך מלא" | "סוף סוף עשיתי קאט אי לבד! הנה איך:" | Tutorial step-by-step |
| "כמה זמן מחזיק הגל?" | "אם הגל נשבר אחרי 4 ימים — זו הסיבה:" | Before/after cracked nail |

**Caption structure:** Hook (1 line) → Explanation (2–4 bullets) → Soft CTA ("הערכה בביו")

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC BEST PRACTICES

### Instagram Reels
- **Hebrew audio:** Always. Even if showing hands only, add Hebrew voiceover or text
- **Hook in first 2 frames:** "ידעת ש..." or "טעות שכולן עושות..."
- **Text overlay:** Large font, RTL, contrasting colors (navy on white or gold on blush)
- **Hashtags:** 5–8 only. #ציפורניים #גלנייל #נייל #מניקור #ציפורניםביתיות #naildrill #nailart
- **Best posting times (Israel):** Tuesday–Friday 6–9pm, Sunday 10am–12pm
- **Collab posts:** Partner with @nails_by_lielll or similar — double the reach

### TikTok
- **Use trending Israeli sounds** (not just global trending — search Israeli trending sounds)
- **Authenticity > production quality** — iPhone vertical video outperforms studio
- **First 1–2 seconds are everything:** "הנה איך עשיתי קאט אי לבד 🤩" then immediate visual
- **Post frequency:** 5–7x/week minimum to grow organically
- **Hebrew captions:** Use Hebrew in the caption AND as TikTok text overlay
- **Best posting times (Israel):** 7–9am, 12–1pm, 7–10pm Sunday–Thursday

### WhatsApp Broadcast
**Setup:** Use WhatsApp Business. Build broadcast list from order confirmations.

**Monthly broadcast cadence:**
- Day 1 of month: Nail trend of the month ("הטרנד הנוכחי — קאט אי כסוף")
- Day 15: Tutorial link + tip
- Holiday: Seasonal offer

**Tone:** Casual, like a friend texting. "שלום! הנה טיפ קטן לציפורניים השבוע 💅"

**Critical rule:** Never spam. Maximum 4 broadcasts/month. Always offer opt-out ("לביטול השלחה — כתבי הסר").

## ENGAGEMENT TACTICS

1. **Comment first strategy:** Engage with 10 Israeli nail accounts BEFORE posting — algorithm boost
2. **Reply within 2 hours:** Every comment on the first 2 hours determines reach (algorithm signal)
3. **Story polls:** "מה הטרנד הבא שאת רוצה?" → generates comments + data on what content to create
4. **Tag and mention:** Credit every customer whose photo you repost
5. **DM engagement:** When someone saves your Reel, send a DM: "שמחה שאהבת! יש שאלות על הערכה?"
6. **Question CTA:** Every caption that ends with a question gets 2x comments

## INFLUENCER STRATEGY

### Micro-influencer targets (5K–50K followers, >2% ER)

| Account | Followers | ER | Content | Approach |
|---------|----------|-----|---------|---------|
| @nails_by_lielll | 15.2K | 2.37% | Home nail tutorials, gel | Gift Kit 2 + ₪300 for 1 post |
| @khaleesi_ellen_rabino | 7.3K | 2.67% | Nail art, stamping | Gift Kit 3 + ₪200 for 1 post |
| Search: Israeli nail accounts 5K–20K | — | >2% | Hebrew tutorials | Systematic outreach |

**Outreach message template:**
> "שלום [שם]! אני מנהלת חנות ישראלית לכלי ציפורניים עם CE מאושר. אני רוצה לשלוח לך את ערכת הסטודיו הביתי — בחינם — בתמורה לפוסט אחד אותנטי. מה דעתך?"

**What to ask for:**
- 1 Reel showing unboxing + first use result
- Tag the brand + use the hashtag
- Authentic opinion (not scripted — genuine reactions convert better)

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't ask for 5 posts from one influencer (too transactional)
- Don't script the entire caption (it reads fake)
- Don't use mega-influencers (>100K) — too expensive, lower engagement rate

## SOCIAL MEDIA + AD SYNERGY

Organic content should fuel paid ads:
1. Post organically for 48–72 hours
2. Check which Reels get highest saves rate (>3%)
3. "Boost" those Reels as Meta Advantage+ ads
4. UGC and testimonial content = best performing ad creative (authentic > studio)
5. WhatsApp broadcast list = warm retargeting audience (upload as Custom Audience in Meta)
