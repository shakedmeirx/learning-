## CONTEXT: E-COMMERCE RETENTION (NOT SAAS)

The /retention-churn-prevention framework is adapted here for a Shopify dropshipping store rather than a SaaS product. "Churn" = never purchasing again. "Retention" = repeat purchase + referral. Key difference: no monthly subscription to cancel, so prevention focus is on re-engagement before the 90-day window closes.

---

## CHURN TYPES FOR THIS BUSINESS

| Type | Definition | Signal | % of Customers |
|------|-----------|--------|----------------|
| **One-and-done** | Bought once, never returned | No second purchase within 90 days | Est. 60–70% at launch |
| **Silent churn** | Stopped engaging before purchasing again | No email open, no WhatsApp response | Est. 15–20% |
| **Consumables forgetter** | Needs refills but buys elsewhere or forgets | No consumables order after 60 days | Est. 20–30% |
| **Outcome failure** | Product didn't work as expected → no rebuy | Support ticket + no second purchase | Est. 5–10% |

**Primary risk for this business:** One-and-done is the dominant pattern for beauty tools. The average beauty tool customer buys once and never returns unless proactively nurtured. The goal is to flip this pattern.

## CUSTOMER HEALTH SCORING (Adapted for E-commerce)

| Signal | Weight | Healthy | At Risk |
|--------|--------|---------|---------|
| Post-purchase WhatsApp response | 25% | Replied to Day 1 message | Never replied |
| Email open rate (welcome sequence) | 20% | Opens 3+ of 5 emails | Opens 0–1 |
| Time-to-first-result | 20% | Messaged "I tried it!" within 14 days | Silence at 14 days |
| Support contact | 15% | 1 question answered ≤ 24hrs | Ticket unresolved >48hrs |
| Review submission | 10% | Left a review | Never left review |
| Second purchase | 10% | Consumables within 60 days | No activity at 60 days |

**Scoring rule:**
- Health score 70–100 → Advocacy campaign (ask for review, referral)
- Health score 40–69 → Engagement nurture (send tip, tutorial, ask how it's going)
- Health score 0–39 → Win-back campaign (acknowledge + offer incentive)

## ONBOARDING (TIME-TO-VALUE — MOST CRITICAL)

Research from retention-churn-prevention framework: **time to first value** predicts long-term retention more than any other variable. For nail tools, "first value" = successful first result.

**Target:** Customer achieves first successful nail result within 14 days of delivery.

### Onboarding Sequence (WhatsApp + Email)

| Day | Channel | Message | Goal |
|-----|---------|---------|------|
| 0 (order confirmation) | WhatsApp | Tracking + "ציפי ל-X–Y ימים" | Anxiety reduction |
| 3 | WhatsApp | "בדרך! הנה 3 דברים לדעת לפני שמתחילים" + video | Pre-arrival prep |
| Delivery day | WhatsApp | "הגיע? הנה סרטון step-by-step" | First use success |
| Day 2 post-delivery | Email | Welcome video series — email 1 | Education |
| Day 7 post-delivery | WhatsApp | "שבוע עם הערכה — טיפ שמשנה הכל" | Engagement |
| Day 14 post-delivery | WhatsApp | "כבר ניסית? שלחי לנו תמונה!" | Social proof + UGC |

**KPI:** % of customers who send a photo/result within 14 days. Target: 25%+.

## RETENTION LEVERS (POST-ONBOARDING)

### 1. WhatsApp Broadcast Nurture (4x/month max)
Monthly nurture keeps the brand top-of-mind:

| Timing | Message | CTA |
|--------|---------|-----|
| Day 1 of month | "הטרנד של החודש: [trend name]" + how-to video | "נסי את זה ↗" |
| Day 15 | "טיפ טכני: איך לטפל בראשי המקדחה" | "צפי בסרטון" |
| Holiday | Seasonal offer (₪30 off consumables) | "הזמיני עכשיו" |
| Seasonal | "חדש: [new product or kit]" | "ראי מה חדש" |

### 2. Consumables Reminder Flow
Triggered by date (60 days post-purchase):

**Email — Day 60:**
> Subject: "הגיע הזמן לחידוש מלאי? 💅"
> Body: "רוב לקוחות ערכה X מגיעות ל-100 פסי שיוף לאחר חודשיים. הנה המלאי שלנו ← [link]"
> Offer: Free shipping on consumables orders over ₪50

### 3. Tutorial Series (Instagram Saves = Retention Signal)
Instagram saves rate predicts repeat purchase intent. Content that drives saves:
- "3 עיצובים שאפשר לעשות עם ערכה בסיסית"
- "טכניקת קאט אי — מדריך שלב אחרי שלב"
- "איך להאריך את חיי הגל שלך ל-3 שבועות"

**Rule:** Every post ends with "שמרי לאוחר ← 🔖" CTA. Saves = the most valuable engagement metric for algorithm + retention.

### 4. Community Building (Long-term)
At 50+ customers: Create a private WhatsApp group "הסטודיו הביתי — קהילת הנייל"
- Members share tips, results, questions
- Brand monitors for product feedback
- UGC source: members naturally share photos
- Churn prevention: community members are 2–3x less likely to stop buying (SaaS benchmark adapted to e-comm)

## REFERRAL PROGRAM DESIGN

### Mechanics
**Program name:** "שתפי את הסטודיו 💅"

| Action | Reward (Referrer) | Reward (Referred) |
|--------|------------------|--------------------|
| Friend makes first purchase | ₪30 store credit | ₪30 off first order |
| Friend buys Kit 2+ | ₪50 store credit | 10% off |

### Implementation (Shopify)
- Use **Referral Candy** or **Smile.io** Shopify app
- Referral link generated per customer in their account
- WhatsApp share button on thank-you page: "שתפי עם חברה ← שניכן תקבלו ₪30"

### Referral Ask Timing
- Day 14 post-delivery WhatsApp: "אהבת? שתפי עם חברה שתאהב גם היא 💅 שניכן מקבלות ₪30"
- Email subject: "הכרנות שוות ₪30 לכל אחת"

## CLV MAXIMIZATION PATH

### Customer LTV Journey (Target 18-month)

```
Month 0:   First purchase (Kit 1 or Kit 2)           → ₪149–449
Month 2:   Consumables restock (sanding bands)        → ₪39
Month 3:   Second consumables or accessory            → ₪50
Month 4:   Kit upgrade (Kit 1 → 2 or Kit 2 → 3)     → ₪150–200 delta
Month 6:   Seasonal purchase or gift for friend       → ₪149–449
Month 8:   Consumables subscription (if launched)     → ₪39/month
18-Month CLV Target: ₪437+
```

| Channel | Avg First Order | Repeat Rate | 12-Month LTV |

**Key insight:** Referral and WhatsApp-organic customers have 2x the repeat rate of paid cold traffic. Invest in referral program early — the ROI compounds.
