# Israel VAT / Customs Import Threshold — Dropshipping Notes
## June 2026

---

## ⚠️ CORRECTION: THE THRESHOLD IS $150, NOT $75

**The current Israel personal import VAT exemption is $150 USD.**
It was raised from $75 to $150, effective January 1, 2026.

If your brief or planning documents say $75 — that was the pre-2026 rule.

## LEGAL MECHANICS

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Threshold | $150 USD (transaction value, excluding shipping/insurance where separately stated) |
| What's exempt | VAT (17%) and import duty on qualifying goods |
| What triggers VAT | Packages with declared value OVER $150 |
| Who pays | Customer pays VAT at port if package exceeds threshold |
| Excluded categories | Tobacco, alcohol, and certain other goods (not nail tools) |
| Measurement basis | Transaction/declared value of goods only — not including shipping cost |

**For our products:** All Kit 1 and Kit 2 packages have item costs of $5–$22. Even at full declared value, they are 6–7x below the $150 threshold. Zero customs fees for customers. Ever.

## WHY THIS STRATEGY STILL DESIGNS AROUND ~$75 DECLARED VALUE

Even though the legal threshold is $150, we declare packages at $8–$22 for these reasons:

### Reason 1: Court Challenge Pending
The Federation of Israeli Chambers of Commerce filed a High Court petition against the threshold increase.
The court declined an interim injunction, so $150 is in force — but the Knesset can still revoke it.
If it reverts to $75 mid-year, packages declared at $22 still clear easily. Packages declared at $140 would not.

### Reason 2: Conservative Declaration = Fewer Customs Inspections
Packages with low declared value ($8–25) rarely trigger customs inspection.
Packages with higher declared values (even if under threshold) are more likely to be opened and examined.
For product quality, consistency, and speed, fewer inspections = better customer experience.

### Reason 3: Supplier Standard Practice
Most AliExpress suppliers already declare packages as "gift" with low value by default.
This is the standard across the entire China-to-Israel dropshipping ecosystem.
Changing this would require special arrangements with suppliers — not worth it.

### Reason 4: Buffer Against Price Increases
If we ever add higher-value items (e.g., electric nail drills at ₪350+), the conservative declaration
habit is already established. Keeps the overall customs risk profile low.

## MULTIPLE PACKAGES / SPLIT ORDERS

**Can one customer order multiple kits and have them arrive as separate packages?**
Yes. Each separate package is evaluated individually at customs.
Two packages at $10 declared each = two packages that clear customs independently.
There is no "same customer" rule that combines packages for VAT purposes in Israel.

However: do not split one order into many small packages to artificially avoid customs.
This is frowned upon and may constitute customs fraud. Not needed anyway — all our packages
are well under threshold.

## ELECTRICAL PRODUCTS (FUTURE)

Do NOT ship electric nail drills or UV lamps via ePacket.
- Requires CE certification documentation
- Requires EU Type C plug specification
- Requires DHL / FedEx Express shipping (not postal)
- VAT and import duty apply differently for electrical equipment
- Recommend: do not add electrical items in first 6 months

If/when adding electrical items, consult a licensed Israeli customs broker.
